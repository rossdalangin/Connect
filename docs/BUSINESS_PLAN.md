# SaaS Business Plan & Positioning Strategy

## 1. Positioning Strategy
**Unique Selling Proposition (USP):** "The all-in-one digital identity and high-conversion lead engine for the modern professional."
Unlike Linktree (just links) or Kontak (just contact info), we combine identity + conversion + CRM in one mobile-first page.

**Target Market Expansion:**
- **Coaches/Consultants:** Capture leads and book calls directly from Instagram/TikTok.
- **Freelancers:** Showcase portfolio links and collect project inquiries.
- **Real Estate Agents:** NFC-ready cards that instantly save contact info and show new listings.
- **Public Figures/Politicians:** Centralized hub for news, socials, and constituent inquiries.

## 2. Offer Structure
- **Free Tier:** 1 Profile, 5 Links, Basic Analytics, "Powered by" branding.
- **Pro Tier ($19/mo):** Unlimited Links, Lead Gen Form, Custom Themes, No Branding, Priority Support.
- **Agency Tier ($99/mo):** Manage up to 10 Profiles, Team Access, Webhook Integrations, API Access.
