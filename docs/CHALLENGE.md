# The 30-Day "First $1,000" SaaS Roadmap

This challenge is for the owner of this SaaS platform. Follow this day-by-day to hit your first $1,000 in monthly recurring revenue (MRR).

## Week 1: Foundation & Beta (Days 1-7)
- **Day 1:** Deploy the platform and generate system pages.
- **Day 2:** Create 5 demo profiles using our **Elite Templates** (Real Estate, Coach, etc.).
- **Day 3:** Reach out to 10 friends. Give them "Founding Member" access (Free Pro for life) in exchange for using it in their bios.
- **Day 5:** Collect feedback. Fix any "friction" in their setup process.
- **Day 7:** **The Reveal:** Post a video of your friends' new high-converting profiles on your own social media.

## Week 2: Niche Penetration (Days 8-15)
- **Day 8:** Pick a niche (e.g., Local Real Estate Agents).
- **Day 9:** Scrape/find 50 agents in your area.
- **Day 10:** Send 25 DMs using the **Cold DM Script** from `docs/MARKETING.md`.
- **Day 12:** Offer a "Bio Audit" call. 15 mins to show them how to increase their leads.
- **Day 15:** Goal: Close 5 Pro users at $19/mo. ($95 MRR).

## Week 3: Automation & Content (Days 16-23)
- **Day 16:** Start the **30-Day Content Plan** (Day 1-7 content).
- **Day 18:** Set up an affiliate program. Give your first Pro users 30% commission for referrals.
- **Day 20:** Run a "Profile Optimization" workshop live on Instagram or LinkedIn.
- **Day 23:** Goal: 15 Pro users. ($285 MRR).

## Week 4: Scaling the Machine (Days 24-30)
- **Day 24:** Reach out to 5 micro-influencers (10k-50k followers). Offer them an Elite partnership.
- **Day 26:** Launch a "Refer a Friend" contest.
- **Day 28:** Double down on the best performing content from Week 3.
- **Day 30:** **The $1k Push:** Run a 48-hour "Launch Flash Sale" ($9/mo for the first year).
- **Day 31:** **GOAL REACHED:** 50-60 paying users. (~$1,000 MRR).

---

## 💡 Pro Tip for Growth:
Focus on the **Leads Captured** stat. When you show a user that they got a lead they would have otherwise lost, you have a customer for life.
