# Elite Funnel Builder Strategy

This document outlines how to use the SaaS Profile system as a multi-step conversion funnel.

## 1. The Anatomy of a High-Converting Profile Funnel
A "Funnel Profile" differs from a standard "Link List" by having a singular primary objective.

### Top of Funnel (Discovery)
- **Visuals:** Use the **Cover Banner** to show a lifestyle image or a "Result" image (e.g., a happy client or a finished project).
- **Headline:** Use an "Outcome-Based" headline: *"I help [Niche] achieve [Result] in [Timeframe]."*
- **Bio:** 2-3 sentences max. Sentence 1: Authority. Sentence 2: The Problem you solve. Sentence 3: Call to Action.

### Middle of Funnel (Engagement/Trust)
- **Video Block:** Embed a YouTube/Vimeo video. Title: *"Watch the Case Study"* or *"How it works."*
- **Testimonial Blocks:** Add at least 3 testimonials below the video.
- **Featured Link:** Use a **Pulse/Glow** animation on your most important link (e.g., your Booking link).

### Bottom of Funnel (Conversion)
- **Lead Form Block:** Place this at the bottom or near the top as a "Lead Magnet" capture.
- **Lead Magnet:** Offer a PDF, Checklist, or Video. Configure the **Lead Automation** tab in the dashboard to automatically deliver the file URL upon submission.

---

## 2. Advanced Funnel Routing
Use **Conditional Routing** (Pro Feature) to segment your traffic:

- **Mobile Users:** Route to an App Store or a mobile-optimized checkout.
- **Geo-Targeting:** Route visitors from different countries to different currency pricing tables or translated landing pages.

---

## 3. Retargeting (The "Hidden" Funnel)
Pro users can add **Meta Pixels** or **Google Analytics** tags.
- **Strategy:** Create a custom audience of people who visited your profile but didn't submit the Lead Form.
- **Ad Copy:** *"I saw you checked out my profile but didn't grab the [Lead Magnet] yet. Here's a direct link to the guide."*
