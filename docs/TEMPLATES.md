# SaaS Profile Templates (Block Sets)

Use these pre-built configurations to maximize conversion for your specific niche.

## 1. The Coach Funnel (High Authority)
**Goal:** Capture high-quality consultation leads.
1. **Header Block:** Large profile pic, "Authority Statement" headline.
2. **Video Block:** "Watch my 2-minute intro video."
3. **Testimonial Block:** 3x client success stories.
4. **FAQ Block:** "How much is the program?" / "Is this for me?"
5. **Button Block:** "Book a Free Discovery Call" (Priority CTA).
6. **Lead Funnel Block:** "Apply for my coaching program."

## 2. The Freelancer Portfolio (Visual Proof)
**Goal:** Showcase work and get project inquiries.
1. **Header Block:** Minimal, creative headline.
2. **Image Gallery Block:** 6x high-quality project screenshots.
3. **Button Block:** "View full portfolio on Behance/GitHub."
4. **Testimonial Block:** 2x client quotes.
5. **Lead Funnel Block:** "Inquire for your project."

## 3. The Local Business / Realtor (NFC-Ready)
**Goal:** Instant contact sharing and listing views.
1. **Header Block:** Logo, "Serving [Area Name]" headline.
2. **Button Block:** "💾 Save My Contact Details" (vCard download - Fixed).
3. **Button Block:** "📞 Call Me Now" (Tel link).
4. **Button Block:** "🏠 View New Listings" (External URL).
5. **Lead Funnel Block:** "Get a free property valuation."
6. **Social Icons Block:** WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram.

## 4. The Politician / Public Service (Centralized Hub)
**Goal:** Disseminate information and gather constituent feedback.
1. **Header Block:** Professional headshot, "Your Voice in [City]" headline.
2. **Video Block:** "Latest town hall update."
3. **Button Block:** "📰 Sign up for my newsletter."
4. **Button Block:** "🗓 Upcoming Events" (Calendar Block).
5. **FAQ Block:** "Where do I stand on [Issue X]?"
6. **Lead Funnel Block:** "Submit your feedback/inquiry."

---

## 🎨 Implementation Note
In the Dashboard, when a user selects a template, the system should automatically populate the Profile with these blocks using the `saas_add_link` AJAX handler with pre-set titles and types.
