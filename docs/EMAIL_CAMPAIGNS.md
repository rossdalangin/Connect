## 2. Cold Outreach (LinkedIn/FB Groups) - "The Helper"

"Hey [Name], just wanted to reach out because I loved your recent post on [Topic]. I'm a consultant too and built a tool specifically for us to keep things high-converting. Mind if I send over a quick 1-min video of how I’d structure your bio for better leads?
